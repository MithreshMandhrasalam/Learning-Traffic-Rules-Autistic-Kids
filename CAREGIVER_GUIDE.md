# 🌻 The Caregiver's Guide to Learning Traffic Rules

Welcome! If you are reading this, you are likely a parent, teacher, or caregiver for a child on the autism spectrum. We know that teaching everyday life skills can be challenging, and we are so glad you are here. 

Your child is a unique, special creature with their own beautiful way of seeing the world. This app was built specifically to embrace how they learn—with patience, predictability, and gentle support.

This simple guide will show you how to use this app alongside your child to teach them about road safety.

---

## 🚦 How the App is Structured

The application is broken down into four main areas. Here is how you can use each one:

### 1. 📘 Learn Mode
**What it is:** A visual dictionary of common traffic signs and rules.
**How to use it:** 
- Sit with your child and explore one or two signs a day. 
- Click on a picture. The app will visually display the rule, and you can press the **🔊 Read Aloud** button so the app will speak the rule in a calm voice. 
- *Tip for Caregivers:* Praise them when they repeat the words or point to the matching shape.

### 2. 🚦 Scenario Simulation (Real-World Practice)
**What it is:** A step-by-step interactive story showing how to cross the road.
**How to use it:**
- This is a "Social Story" brought to life. Social stories are incredibly helpful for autistic children to understand sequence and expectations.
- Press the button to move from Red Light (Stop) ➡ Yellow Light (Hold Hands) ➡ Green Light (Cross).
- *Tip for Caregivers:* Practice this simulation indoors first, acting it out in the living room, before trying it on a real sidewalk.

### 3. 📝 Quiz Mode
**What it is:** A pressure-free, gentle quiz to reinforce what they have learned.
**How to use it:**
- There is no timer and no failing grade. If they get it wrong, the app gently encourages them to try again.
- Use the **🔊 Read Question** button if reading text is overwhelming for them.
- *Tip for Caregivers:* Celebrate every right answer! Let the app say "Great job!" and follow it up with a high-five.

### 4. 👶 Profile
**What it is:** A visual representation of their progress.
**How to use it:**
- Many children on the spectrum love collecting items or seeing visual proof of their achievements.
- *Tip for Caregivers:* Use this page as a reward system. "Let's learn two more signs today to add to your profile!"

## 💛 A Note of Support for You

Being a caretaker requires endless patience, immense love, and deep emotional strength. It is okay if some days are harder than others. It is okay if a lesson needs to be repeated ten times. 

You are providing moral and emotional support to a wonderful child who relies on your guidance. You are their safe harbor. Use this app as a tool to make your day just a little bit easier, and remember that every small step forward is a massive victory.

You are doing a fantastic job. 💙
